ABOUT WEN MINT?!

Welcome to WEN MINT?! — a playful auction simulator inspired by the Pocket Dragons community.

Practice your bidding strategy, compete against virtual Keepers, discover how the clearing price works, and try to win your eggs without blowing your virtual budget.

Choose your difficulty, listen to our mischievous auction host, and see if you can survive the bidding madness!

No real money. No wallet connection. Just virtual eggs, chaotic Keepers, and a little auction madness.

An independent community-made fan game inspired by Pocket Dragons. Not an official Pocket Dragons auction or mint.
